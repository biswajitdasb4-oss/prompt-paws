# The panda's concept prompt

Made with GPT Image 2.5 through Higgsfield: quality high, 2K, 3:4. Result: `concept.png`.

Base prompt (OUTFIT replaced by the outfit line below):

> Original character design: a chubby, friendly giant panda, stylised 3D animated-film character with a
> high-end feature-animation look, soft fluffy black-and-white fur, big warm expressive eyes, gentle smile,
> round tummy, short sturdy legs, big soft paws. OUTFIT. Full body, front view, facing the camera straight on,
> standing in a relaxed A-pose: both arms held out about 30 degrees away from the body, open relaxed hands
> with visible fingers, legs slightly apart, both feet flat and fully visible, the whole character visible
> from the top of the head to the feet. Nothing in his hands, no cape, no props. Plain light grey background,
> soft even studio lighting, no cast shadows, no floor line. Clean high detail. No text, no logo, no watermark.

Outfit used ("cozy coder"):

> He wears a soft mustard-yellow knitted hoodie with the hood down, big over-ear headphones resting around
> his neck, and dark grey jogger trousers

Two other outfits were written but not generated: an explorer (khaki shirt, cargo shorts, wide-brim hat)
and a painter (navy beret, white smock with paint splashes).

Why the prompt is shaped this way: the picture feeds an automatic 3D and rigging step. A front view in an
A-pose with empty hands rigs cleanly. No cape (auto-rigged cloth does not move on its own), no long props
(they confuse the rigger), no dangling scarf or bag (it hangs frozen). Avoid the word "bare" (image filters
misread it).
